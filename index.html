Interactive Mechanics

How It Works

Endless Story Mode

Sit back and enjoy the infinite story loop. Arthur and Bob never teleport; they enter and leave only through their own doors. Bob’s shutter rolls up and down, and Arthur’s window light turns off when he sleeps.

Play as the Kids

Take direct control! Click or tap anywhere to throw your selected projectile at the lamp. Watch the bulb develop spiderweb cracks before bursting. Once broken, the adult repair sequence runs automatically!

Escalating Variations

As the night wears on, Bob gets increasingly exasperated, Arthur brings out a flashlight on repair #5, and Bob eventually shows up wearing his pillow on repair #7!
